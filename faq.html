Frequently Asked Questions

What is AcePaste?

AcePaste is a simple browser‑based paste tool that gives you a clean space to drop, format and edit text. It is optimized for speed, privacy and convenience.

Why would I use a paste tool?

Copying text often brings unwanted formatting, weird spacing or hidden characters. AcePaste acts as a clean staging area so you can inspect or fix text before using it elsewhere.

Does AcePaste save my text or upload it anywhere?

No. Everything happens locally in your browser. Your text never leaves your device, and nothing is stored or logged.

Is AcePaste free?

Yes. AcePaste is free to use.
