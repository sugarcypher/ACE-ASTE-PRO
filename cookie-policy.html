Cookie Policy

Last updated: May 2026 · ThinkWell Labs LLC

The short version

Ace Paste Cleaner Pro does not use tracking cookies, analytics cookies, or advertising cookies. We do not track you across the web. We do not sell your data. We do not run ads.

What we actually use

When you sign in to your account, we store your session in sessionStorage — a temporary browser store that is automatically cleared when you close the tab. It never persists to disk as a cookie and is never sent to third parties.

When you use the browser extension, your subscription status is stored locally in chrome.storage.local — on your device only, never uploaded.

We use Cloudflare Turnstile on the sign-in and sign-up forms as an invisible bot-detection challenge. Turnstile may set a short-lived technical cookie to validate the challenge. It contains no personal information and is not used for tracking. See Cloudflare's privacy policy for details.

What we do not use

No Google Analytics. No Facebook Pixel. No ad networks. No session replay tools. No heatmaps. No fingerprinting. The text you paste is processed entirely in your browser — it never leaves your device.

Your rights

Under GDPR, CCPA, and similar regulations, you have the right to know what data is collected about you. In our case: beyond your email address (used only to manage your account) and your subscription status, we collect nothing. You can delete your account at any time by emailing privacy@acepaste.xyz.

Changes to this policy

If we ever add tracking or analytics, we will update this page and notify users. We don't anticipate doing so.
