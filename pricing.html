Common questions

Does one subscription really cover both the web app and the extension?

Yes. Sign in with the same email on acepaste.xyz and in the extension. Both check the same backend, so a single active subscription unlocks everything on both surfaces simultaneously.

What happens after I buy a 1-day trial?

Your account is upgraded to full access for 24 hours from the moment of purchase. After that, features revert to the free tier unless you subscribe. The $1.23 is not deducted from any subscription price — it's a standalone access fee.

Can I cancel my subscription?

Yes, at any time from your account page. Monthly billing stops immediately on cancellation; yearly billing stops at the end of your current period. Lifetime purchases never expire.

Does my data leave my browser?

Text you paste and clean is never sent to any server — all cleaning runs locally in your browser. The only data we transmit is your account credentials (email + hashed password) for authentication and a subscription-status check on login. Cleaning is, and will remain, entirely local.

How are the "Save X%" figures calculated?

Each tier uses the metric that best represents its value. Monthly saves 67% versus 30 individual daily purchases ($36.90 total). Annual gives you 2 months free versus renewing monthly for a year ($148.08 total). Lifetime uses breakeven framing — at $123.45/yr, it pays itself off in 23 months and every month after that costs nothing.

What payment methods are accepted?

All major credit and debit cards via Stripe. Stripe also supports Apple Pay and Google Pay where available. We do not store any card details ourselves.
